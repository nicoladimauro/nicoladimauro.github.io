I'm associate professor of Computer Science at DIB, University of Bari "Aldo Moro". My research interests are in the area of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, with particular emphasis on tractable probabilistic models and on methods for structured and relational data. You may find my list of publications on my Google Scholar profile, on the DBLP computer science bibliography, and on this page.
