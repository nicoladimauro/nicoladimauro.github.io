Research

I’m interested in machine learning and its applications. In particular I worked on the following topics:

  • Tractable Probabilistic Models and Deep Learning
  • Statistical Relational Learning: Relational Sequence Learning, Learning LPADs
  • (Cooperative) Clustering-Grouping problems: Coalition Structure Generation
  • Inductive Logic Programming (ILP) and Multi-relational Data Mining: Theory Revision, Incremental Learning, Subsumption procedures, Multistrategy Learning, Abstraction and Abduction procedures, Stochastic Propositionalization, Approximate Reasoning, Relational Sequence Mining, Generalization models, Similarity measures, Discretization, Conceptual Clustering, Order Effects in Incremental Learning, Learning by Imitation, Learning Agent Behaviour
  • Document Image Analysis and Recognition: Machine Learning for Digital Document Processing (document classification, document understanding and layout analysis), Content-based Indexing of Digital Documents
  • Bioinformatics: Machine Learning for Modeling Metabolic Networks, Mining Biologically Active Patterns in Metabolic Pathways, Protein Fold Recognition
